                How it works

                1. Measure. Loudness, peaks, clipping, noise, the balance across eight bands, the tempo and the pitch the dayan rings at — the moment you choose a recording.
                2. Prepare. Trim it, mark a noise-only stretch, and tap on sam to lock the lehra to your playing. A Swaralaya lehra is made at your tempo and Sa.
                3. Repair and shape. Clipped peaks are rebuilt, mains hum notched out and noise lightly reduced; then an EQ and compressor for your instrument, set from what was measured.
                4. Master. Taken to the loudness your platform plays at, with a true peak under −1 dBTP, and checked again on the finished files.
                5. Riyaz. How steady your laya was, how even your strokes, how close to sam — measured, so you can see it across takes.

                The numbers are measurements, not opinions: the EQ values are starting points, so listen to the versions and keep the one that sounds right on your phone. Recordings are deleted from the server after a few hours, or sooner with the button above.

                  Notation Studio Guide

                  1. Adding compositions

                  Click any cell in the grid to select it. Once selected, you can:

                  • Type directly using your keyboard.
                  • Click items in the Palette to insert them.
                  • Use the Templates dropdown in the toolbar to load a sample composition.

                  2. Formatting (vocal & tabla)

                  Select a cell and use the formatting buttons in the toolbar to apply the standard notation marks:

                  • Mandra / Taar: a dot below or above the note.
                  • Komal / Tivra: an underline or a vertical line.
                  • The last button clears the mark.

                  3. Playback

                  Press the play button to hear a synthesized playback of your notation. It reads exactly what you've typed, in the selected language and with its marks.

                  Compositions

                    Saved compositions stay in this browser. A share link carries the whole composition inside it — nothing is uploaded.

                    Studio Effects

                    Turn off if you need the lowest possible latency — the media element adds a small output buffer.

                    Sync the lehra

                    Drag the lehra track to move it. Double-click each track where its sam is (it snaps to the stroke), then Line up sams. Zoom in for the last few milliseconds.
