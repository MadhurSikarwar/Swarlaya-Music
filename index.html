          Notation Studio Guide

          1. Adding compositions

          Click any cell in the grid to select it. Once selected, you can:

          • Type directly using your keyboard.
          • Click items in the Palette to insert them.
          • Use the Templates dropdown in the toolbar to load a sample composition.

          2. Formatting (vocal & tabla)

          Select a cell and use the formatting buttons in the toolbar to apply the standard notation marks:

          • Mandra / Taar: a dot below or above the note.
          • Komal / Tivra: an underline or a vertical line.
          • The last button clears the mark.

          3. Playback

          Press the play button to hear a synthesized playback of your notation. It reads exactly what you've typed, in the selected language and with its marks.
